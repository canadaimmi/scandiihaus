The Scandiihaus Difference

Unparalleled Craftsmanship, Exceptional Value

Scandinavians have gathered around fire-heated water for generations. We build that ritual properly — solid timber, real fire, and joinery that holds up to a Canadian winter.

Working directly with the craftsmen who build our saunas and tubs keeps the price honest. No showroom markup, no middlemen, no plastic shells pretending to be wood.

  • Western red cedar and thermo-treated timber
  • Wood-fired heat — no electrical hookup needed
  • Delivered assembled and ready to site
  • Planning help before you commit to a model
