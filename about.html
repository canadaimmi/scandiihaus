Our Story

Why We Started

A Ritual Worth Bringing Home

Scandiihaus began with a simple frustration: the wood fired hot tubs we loved on trips to the Nordics were nearly impossible to find back home — or came with a price tag and lead time that put them out of reach.

So we set out to build them ourselves, working directly with Scandinavian craftsmen who have shaped wood for fire and water for generations. The result is a tub that costs a fraction of custom-built alternatives, without compromising on the materials or the experience.

How We Build

Real Wood. Real Fire. No Shortcuts.

Every Scandiihaus tub is built from rot-resistant Scandinavian spruce, finished with non-toxic sealants safe for skin and water, and fitted with an external wood-fired stove engineered for efficient, even heating.

  • ✓ No electrical hookup, ever
  • ✓ Hand-fitted joinery, no particleboard
  • ✓ Tested for four-season Canadian winters
