Our Story

Why We Started

A Ritual Worth Bringing Home

Scandiihaus began with a straightforward frustration. The saunas and wood-fired tubs we had used on trips through the Nordics were almost impossible to buy back home — and the few that were available carried a price tag and a lead time that put them out of reach for most people.

So we went to the source. We work directly with the craftsmen who cut, bend and fit these buildings, which means no importer margin, no showroom overhead, and no plastic shell dressed up as timber.

What arrives at your gate is the thing itself: cedar, thermo-treated pine, steel banding, a stove and a door. Assembled, tested, and ready for the first fire.

How We Build

Real Wood. Real Fire. No Shortcuts.

Every Cedarhavn barrel is cut from clear western red cedar and banded in steel. Every Nordhavn Haus is clad in charred timber and lined in knotless pine. Our hot tubs use an external wood-fired stove that heats by convection — no pump, no control panel, nothing to fail in a cold snap.

  • Clear western red cedar and thermo-treated pine
  • Hand-fitted joinery, no particleboard anywhere
  • Non-toxic sealants, safe against skin and water
  • Tested through full Canadian winters

What We Stand For

Our Values

Sustainably Sourced

Timber comes from responsibly managed northern forests, replanted as it is harvested. We would rather wait for the right cedar than substitute something cheaper.

Honestly Priced

Buying direct from the workshop takes a layer of margin out of the price. We pass that on rather than spending it on a showroom you would never visit.

Built to Last

Backed by a five-year structural warranty and decades of Nordic woodworking practice. These are built to be handed on, not replaced.
